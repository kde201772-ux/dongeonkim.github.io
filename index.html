I study colloidal quantum dot (CQD) optoelectronics, focusing on PbS QD synthesis, surface passivation, and device fabrication for shortwave infrared (SWIR) photodetectors and photovoltaics. Member of NECO Lab, Dept. of Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering.

Research

Colloidal Quantum Dots
⬡

CQD Synthesis & Passivation

PbS, InAs, and Ag₂Te quantum dot synthesis. Surface ligand exchange strategies for improved carrier transport and stability.

◈

SWIR Photodetectors

High-performance shortwave infrared photodetectors including avalanche photodiodes (APDs) and broadband Vis–SWIR detectors.

◉

Photovoltaics

Solution-processed solar cells leveraging quantum confinement. Thermophotovoltaics and perovskite tandem architectures.

◫

Device Modeling & ML

TCAD optical simulations, Transfer Matrix Method, and Bayesian machine learning for QD synthesis optimization.
